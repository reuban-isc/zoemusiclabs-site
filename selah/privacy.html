SELAH  REVERB

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026. Applies to the Selah app and its Audio Unit on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Selah collects no data

Selah does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any information about you or how you use it. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting services and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own.

What stays on your device

  • Audio. When you turn on Live in, Selah uses the microphone or your audio interface to play your sound through the reverb. The audio is processed in memory as you play. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your presets and settings. Presets you save, your favourites, MIDI mappings and app settings are kept on your device, shared only between the Selah app and its Audio Unit. When Selah runs in a host app, the host saves Selah's settings inside your project, as it does for every plug-in.
  • Preset files you import. Selah reads only the file you choose.

Permissions

  • Microphone: asked only when you choose Live in.
  • Bluetooth (iPhone and iPad): used only when you pair a Bluetooth MIDI controller in Selah's Settings.
  • Local network (iPhone and iPad): used only when you turn on Network MIDI in Selah's Settings, so another device on your Wi-Fi can send MIDI to Selah. While it is on, any device on that network can connect, so turn it off on shared Wi-Fi. MIDI is the only thing Selah accepts over it.

You can turn any of these off at any time in your device's Settings.

Apple

Apple handles your purchase and may share anonymous sales figures and, if you opt in to share them with developers, crash reports with us. Apple's own privacy policy covers that.

Children

Selah is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
